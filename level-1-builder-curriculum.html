The operating principle

Protect the floor; raise the ceiling. Keep your job and essential expenses first. Study through small, shippable projects. Start applying when you have proof and a clear role story, not when you feel “fully ready.” Salary ranges are direction, never a promise: location, experience, interview performance, and the market matter.

*Income target: ₹60–100k/month is an aspirational 6–12 month search target from the referenced conversation, not a guaranteed outcome. Keep applying to safe-fit roles while learning; don't wait for month 12 to explore the market.

Workload & recovery protocol

Consistency beats heroic catch-up.

Minimum week · 4hOne lesson, one small build step, one reflection. Use when work or life is heavy.
Standard · 6–8hNormal target: 3 short sessions + 3–4h build block.
Deep week · 8–12hOnly when energy and deadlines justify it. Never treat this as the baseline.
Miss 1 weekResume normally. Do not double the next week.
Miss 2 weeksTake a reset week: review, finish one small proof, then resume.
Miss 3–4+ weeksRebase the route. Drop low-value tasks; keep the core sequence and career engine.

Rules that protect your downside

  • Don't resign for a speculative idea.
  • Don't spend heavily on courses or APIs. Set a small monthly cap.
  • Never put secrets, customer data, or employer material in public demos.
  • Build with fake or permissioned data; get written permission before using work examples.
  • Prefer a role step that compounds your skills and income over a glamorous title.
